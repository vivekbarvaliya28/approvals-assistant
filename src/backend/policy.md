# Content Approval Policy

## Purpose
Every asset shown to patrol operators must be reviewed before publication. Reviews protect operators from outdated instructions, unsafe guidance and exposure of sensitive site information.

## Who Can Approve
Only users with the Approver role may approve or reject content. Submitters can never approve their own uploads. If you submitted an item, ask another Approver to review it.

## Review Timeframes
Standard items must be reviewed within three business days. Items marked safety-critical, such as equipment specifications or emergency procedures, must be reviewed within one business day. Items pending longer than five business days are escalated to the Operations Manager automatically.

## Checklist by Content Type
Folders: confirm every file inside is current, correctly named and free of duplicates.
Videos: watch the full video, verify the narration matches current procedures, and check that no faces, licence plates or restricted areas are visible.
PDFs: check the document version, the effective date and that all specification values match the manufacturer's data.
Images: confirm the image is legible, correctly labelled, and that camera positions and zone boundaries match the real site.

## Approving and Rejecting
Approve only when every checklist item passes. Reject with a written reason of at least one sentence so the submitter knows what to fix. Never approve an item you have not opened. Partial approvals are not allowed; resubmit the corrected item instead.

## Escalation
Escalate to the Operations Manager when content contains personal data, conflicts with an existing procedure, or when you are unsure. Do not guess.

## Audit Trail
Every decision is logged with the reviewer, timestamp and reason. Decisions cannot be edited after submission; a reversal requires a new review by a different Approver.
